# Dataset notes

- Dataset name: Gallstone dataset (UCI Machine Learning Repository)
- Source URL: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/1150/gallstone-1
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- Citation / DOI: https://doi.org/10.1097/md.0000000000037258
- Original paper: Esen et al. (2024), "Early prediction of gallstone disease with a machine learning-based method from bioimpedance and laboratory data", Medicine.
- Collected at (hospital, country): Internal Medicine Outpatient Clinic, Ankara VM Medical Park Hospital, Ankara, Turkey
- Collection period: June 2022 to June 2023
- Number of patients (rows): 319 (161 with gallstone disease)
- Number of variables (columns): 38 features + 1 outcome variable
- Outcome variable: gallstone status (yes/no)
- Missing values: none
- Date downloaded: 10 November 2026
- Ethical notes: collection approved by the Ankara City Hospital Ethics Committee (E2-23-4632); data publicly available under CC BY 4.0
